📌 Project Overview
This Power BI project provides a comprehensive retail analytics solution for FoodMart, covering sales, profit, customer behavior, and store performance across 1997 and 1998. It integrates multiple datasets into interactive dashboards, enabling managers, analysts, and decision-makers to monitor business growth, customer profitability, and product trends.

🎯 Objectives
Sales Tracking: Monitor total sales, profit, returns, and losses across years.
Store Performance: Compare sales and profit by store type and size.
Customer Insights: Rank customers based on profitability.
Product Analysis: Identify top-selling products and categories.
Regional Trends: Visualize sales distribution across regions.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Power BI – Interactive reporting and visualization
Data Modeling – Relationships between customers, products, stores, and regions
DAX Measures – Custom KPIs for profit, sales, and rankings
Retail Data Sources – Historical sales, customer, and store datasets

🚀 Use Cases
Business Strategy – Identify profitable regions and stores.
Customer Retention – Track top customers and loyalty trends.
Product Planning – Focus on high-performing products.
Performance Benchmarking – Compare yearly growth and targets.
